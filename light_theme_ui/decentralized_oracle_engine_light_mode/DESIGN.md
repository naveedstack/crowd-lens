## Brand & Style

The design system powers an institutional-grade, decentralized consensus and human content verification engine. Translating high-throughput Web3 telemetry into a pristine light mode, the visual identity rejects decorative crypto noise, stark dark-mode terminal clichés, and blinding glare in favor of clinical clarity, crisp contrast, and analytical confidence.

- **Aesthetic Direction:** High-Clarity Corporate Minimalist meets Technical Web3 Instrumentation. Defined by immaculate white surfaces, cool zinc-slate structure, sharp ink typography, and targeted chromatic signals.
- **Target Audience:** Content curators, validators, protocol engineers, and decentralized governance participants requiring continuous legibility and zero optical fatigue during intensive data evaluation.
- **Emotional Response:** Uncompromising verifiability, cryptographic authority, institutional calm, and speed.

## Colors

The light mode color palette shifts from deep cosmic slate to a high-contrast architectural scheme anchored by crisp white (`#ffffff`), cool canvas slate (`#f8fafc`), and deep slate typography (`#0f172a`).

### Architectural Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base (`surface-canvas`):** `#f8fafc` (Slate 50) forms the glare-free background plane for entire operational canvases.
- **Primary Panel (`surface-card`):** `#ffffff` provides crisp separation for oracle jobs, interactive comparison frames, metric tables, and staking drawers.
- **Recessed Tray (`surface-subtle`):** `#f1f5f9` (Slate 100) hosts nested data grids, inactive field inputs, asset inspection wells, and inactive tab selectors.
- **Structural Outlines (`border-subtle` & `border-strong`):** `#e2e8f0` (Slate 200) sets standard component boundaries; `#cbd5e1` (Slate 300) highlights focused dividers, active borders, and hover outlines.

### Chromatic Identifiers
- **Primary Violet (`#7c3aed`):** Dedicated to primary calls-to-action, final consensus confirmations, and primary signature actions. Hover state drops to `#6d28d9`.
- **Secondary Cyan (`#0284c7` / `#06b6d4`):** Flags cryptographic telemetry, epoch status markers, protocol node addresses, and RPC connection chips.
- **Tertiary & Verified Emerald (`#059669` / `#10b981`):** Applied to consensus rewards, validator yield, settlement balances, and verified assertion states.
- **State Badges:** All contextual feedback uses tinted micro-surfaces (e.g., `#ecfdf5` for success, `#fff1f2` for disputes) paired with crisp solid text and matching border rings to maintain high accessibility in high-ambient light environments.

## Typography

The typography pairings contrast the crisp geometry of Plus Jakarta Sans for structural branding and section headlines with the high optical density of Inter for analytical readings.

- **Header Treatment:** Top-level headlines utilize Plus Jakarta Sans with purposeful tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) rendered in deep slate (`#0f172a`). Prominent hero statements may employ a controlled linear gradient transitioning from `#6d28d9` (Violet 700) to `#0284c7` (Cyan 600) for punchy visual prestige.
- **Tabular Numerics:** For all validator stakes, block heights, epoch timers, and account yields, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` is mandatory to avoid shifting layouts during real-time streaming updates.
- **Dual Currency Structure:** Present reward calculations with precise visual hierarchy:
  - Standard format: Primary fiat value (`$4.50`) in `#0f172a` (Weight 600), split by a neutral center dot (`·` in `#94a3b8`), ending with network currency (`0.037 SOL`) in `#64748b` (Weight 500) or `#7c3aed` when actively claimable.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a standard 12-column responsive fluid grid bounded within a maximum content boundary of `1280px` to maintain optimal ergonomic scanning on wide displays.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid featuring `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) canvas margins. Oracle image evaluation feeds utilize balanced 6/6 side-by-side columns or an 8/4 partition with telemetry and staking modules fixed on the right rail.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters. Image comparisons transition to stacked or tabbed cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid system with `1rem` (16px) margins. Wallet connectors and submission controls lock to an ergonomic persistent bottom bar.

## Elevation & Depth

To prevent the visual glare and muddy look common in light Web3 interfaces, the design system employs crisp low-contrast hairline borders accompanied by diffuse, cool-tinted ambient drop shadows.

- **Base Layer (Canvas):** Pure `#f8fafc` flat background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Containers):** Pure `#ffffff` background with `1px solid #e2e8f0` and an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Flyouts & Modals):** `#ffffff` background with `1px solid #cbd5e1` supported by an elevated soft shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Interactive Focus & Active Staking:** Hairline border switches to `1px solid #7c3aed` with a soft violet focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.15)`.
- **Backdrop Overlays:** Modals and slide-overs use `#0f172a` tinted to 20% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`, preserving light contextual awareness without obscuring hierarchy.

## Shapes

The design system uses a balanced geometric baseline (Level 2: `0.5rem` / 8px) that pairs structural discipline with modern software usability.

- **Cards & Data Modules:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) to house image sets, consensus graphs, and account details.
- **Buttons, Form Inputs, & Control Panels:** Standard `rounded` (`0.5rem` / 8px) for crisp interactive edges.
- **Status Tags, Network Badges, & Wallet Pills:** Full pill (`9999px`) styling to clearly differentiate operational metadata from actionable form blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#7c3aed`, text `#ffffff`, font weight `600`, radius `0.5rem`. Hover shifts to `#6d28d9`; active clicks scale subtly (`transform: scale(0.99)`). Box shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.3)`.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Surface `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, text `#334155`, font weight `500`. Hover brings surface `#f8fafc` and border `#cbd5e1`.
- **Subtle / Ghost:** Surface transparent, text `#334155`. Hover shifts to `#f1f5f9` with text `#0f172a`.
- **Destructive:** Solid `#e11d48`, text `#ffffff`. Hover shifts to `#be123c`.

### Content Oracle Image Comparison Cards
- Enclosed within a white panel with `1px solid #e2e8f0` and `rounded-lg` (`1rem`).
- **Asset Bays:** Nested containers finished in `#f1f5f9` with inset hairline borders (`#e2e8f0`) and subtle image drop shadows. Integrated inspection tools (zoom toggle, cryptographic SHA-256 hash pill, aspect ratio marker) float at the top with translucent `#ffffff` (80% opacity) pill containers.
- **Vote Allocation Trays:** Distinct binary choice buttons ("Option A" / "Option B") anchored below each candidate asset, shifting to `#7c3aed` fill when selected, with dual-currency staking impact dynamically reflected.

### Wallet Control Bar (Header)
- Self-contained pill unit (`rounded-full`) anchored in the top utility nav.
- Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, shadow `0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- Displays active cluster pill (`#ecfeff` background, `#0284c7` text, pulsing cyan dot), truncated Solana address (`#0f172a`, weight 600), and integrated balance counter (`#64748b`).

### Chips & Status Indicators
- **Devnet Status:** Background `#ecfeff`, border `1px solid #a5f3fc`, text `#0284c7`, font weight `600`, full pill.
- **Verified / Yield Accrued:** Background `#ecfdf5`, border `1px solid #a7f3d0`, text `#059669`, font weight `600`, full pill.
- **Pending Quorum:** Background `#fffbeb`, border `1px solid #fde68a`, text `#d97706`, font weight `600`, full pill.
- **Disputed:** Background `#fff1f2`, border `1px solid #fecdd3`, text `#e11d48`, font weight `600`, full pill.

### Form Inputs & Stake Sliders
- Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, text `#0f172a`, placeholder text `#94a3b8`, radius `0.5rem`.
- Focus: Border shifts to `#7c3aed` with `0 0 0 3px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.12)`.
- Sliders: Track is `#e2e8f0` with active track segment in `#7c3aed`; thumb is pure `#ffffff` with `2px solid #7c3aed` and subtle elevation shadow.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `1.125rem` (18px) with `0.25rem` radius (checkbox) or circular (radio).
- Default: Border `1.5px solid #cbd5e1`, background `#ffffff`.
- Selected: Background `#7c3aed`, border `#7c3aed`, displaying pure white check/bullet icon.
