Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about staking, validator pools, and quorum mechanics.

How does the 80% validator reward pool work?expand_more

When a creator submits a task, they escrow $1.00 per required vote in Devnet SOL. Exactly 80% ($0.80 per vote) is locked directly into the voter escrow reward pool. As each vote completes and the quorum reaches finality, the smart contract disperses $0.80 per vote to each validator's wallet automatically.

What is the minimum and maximum quorum size?expand_more

You can deploy tasks requiring anywhere between 10 votes (for quick exploratory checks) up to 1,000 votes (for high-stakes channel uploads or enterprise campaigns). The interactive calculator lets you choose any value within this 10–1,000 range with live escrow updates.

How does CrowdLens prevent bots and Sybil attacks?expand_more

Validators must maintain lightweight Devnet SOL reputation bonding. Anomalous latency, fingerprint similarity, or clicking patterns outside human cognitive thresholds are flagged by the validator graph. Slashing mechanisms remove malicious nodes from consensus pools.

Do validators need to request withdrawals?expand_more

No! CrowdLens has zero balance withdrawal thresholds. As soon as a quorum reaches finality, the escrow contract sends the $0.80 (in Devnet SOL) directly to your connected Phantom wallet.
