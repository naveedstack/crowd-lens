Protocol Integrity

Validator Reputation & Security FAQ

Everything you need to know about slashing conditions, blind commits, and payout cycles.

CrowdLens implements a non-punitive learning curve for new validators. If you vote for an outlier option, you simply forfeit that single ballot’s $0.80 bounty—you are never slashed for honest subjective disagreements. However, persistent random spam botting lowers your validator tier, temporarily suspending ballot eligibility until recalibrated.
To guarantee pure independence, every validator submits a cryptographic SHA-256 hash containing their choice combined with a unique wallet nonce. No one—not even other validators or creators—can inspect the current voting split until the required quorum threshold is reached. Once closed, votes are revealed simultaneously in a single atomic smart contract transaction.
Zero limits. Rewards are programmed directly as Solana Program Library (SPL) token transfers. When a batch completes its quorum validation, the creator escrow smart contract dispatches your $0.80 SOL directly into your connected Phantom address within the same block.
You can utilize the CrowdLens integrated Devnet Faucet in the navigation header, or call the standard Solana CLI faucet via `solana airdrop 2`. While in Devnet mode, validator transactions are sponsored by protocol gas relays, allowing frictionless test evaluations.
